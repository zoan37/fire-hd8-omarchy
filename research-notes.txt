Mobile examples researched after user's iPhone comparison:
https://github.com/modpunk/Omarchy-iPhone6s
Author reports Arch Linux ARM, Hyprland and Omarchy Phone shell on a physical
iPhone 6s (N71/Samsung A9). It uses tethered checkm8/pongoOS/m1n1 boot into
RAM on HoolockLinux, simpledrm, software rendering via Mesa llvmpipe and a
Hyprland/aquamarine glFinish shim. No storage writes; reboot restores iOS.
Touch is not usable, charging unreliable, no Wi-Fi/storage/modem drivers.
This is evidence for a native experimental ARM desktop, not a persistent
complete replacement OS or verified evidence for the claimed iPhone 8.
The useful general strategy is early software rendering after a working
DRM display path. Giza's kernel lacks that display path; llvmpipe alone
does not solve its framebuffer-only integration or old-kernel compatibility.
Apple boot exploits and hardware drivers do not transfer to MediaTek giza.

https://github.com/danieljohnmorris/omarchy-phone
Developer documents a native OnePlus 6T installation replacing Android,
combining Arch Linux ARM, postmarketOS's SDM845 kernel and Omarchy shell
patches. This is a potential userspace/build reference once Fire-specific
boot and display problems are resolved; its firmware/images are not for Fire.
https://danieljohnmorris.com/writing/omarchy-on-a-oneplus-6t/
The article mentions Chris Barnhart's early-September old-iPhone photo,
but does not establish its boot method. Do not assume that photo is a VM,
remote desktop, or complete native install without the original method.
